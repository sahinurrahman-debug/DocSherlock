OPERATIONS POST-MORTEM - CUSTOMER PORTAL OUTAGE
Date: September 20, 2023

TIMELINE
The outage started on September 15, 2023 and lasted 4 hours before the on-call engineer restored service.

ROOT CAUSE
An expired TLS certificate on the tracking API gateway took the portal offline. Certificate auto-renewal was switched off during the cloud migration.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
Operations will review certificate monitoring for all public endpoints.
